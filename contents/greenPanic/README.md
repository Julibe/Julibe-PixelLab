# Green Panic
### Isometric Ghost-Hunting Chaos

## TL;DR Version
Green Panic is an isometric, retro-inspired arcade game where no two runs are exactly the same! You control a nimble hero trapped inside a procedurally generated maze, constantly hunted by relentless ghosts.

To keep things wild, the game actively throws random "Chaos Events" at you, like sudden speed boosts or raining bombs! It uses a strict four-color green palette to deliver maximum nostalgia while leveraging modern HTML5 Canvas for buttery smooth 2.5D rendering. It is a perfect fusion of retro aesthetics and modern roguelike tension!

## Introduction (Welcome to the Grid)
Step into a world where everything is just a little bit greener and a whole lot more dangerous! Green Panic takes the classic arcade maze chase and flips it entirely on its axis. Built from the ground up for the browser, this incredible project is a love letter to the golden era of handheld gaming, injected with the relentless tension of modern procedural design. 🕹️

As a massive lover of sci-fi, anime, and a pro at seeing "Game Over" screens since the 90s, I wanted to build something that captures the magic of late-night gaming sessions under the covers with a flashlight. No bloat, no heavy downloads, just pure code and pure adrenaline. Are you ready to outsmart the AI and survive the chaos? Let us dive right in!

## Project Overview (The Core Loop)
Imagine if the classic yellow dot-muncher got trapped inside a radioactive, shifting matrix. That is Green Panic! The primary goal is simple: survive, score points, and do not get caught. But the unique isometric perspective adds a fresh, exciting layer of spatial awareness, making every single corner turn a thrilling gamble. 👻

The ghosts are not just wandering aimlessly; they are actively hunting you, forcing you to use your dash ability and quick thinking to stay alive! What makes this truly special is the unpredictability. The 'Chaos Events' ensure that you can never just memorize a static pattern. One second you are methodically clearing the board, and the next, you are dodging a literal minefield while running at double speed!

### Inspiration (Roots of the Maze)
The beautiful concept of the maze chase is as old as video games themselves. It gained massive global popularity thanks to [Toru Iwatani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toru_Iwatani), the visionary creator of the original arcade classic. His brilliant idea of appealing to a broader audience through universally recognizable shapes laid the foundation for decades of game design.

However, Green Panic does not just look to the arcades. It heavily draws from the classic [Game Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Boy) era. The hardware constraints of the late 80s forced developers to focus entirely on gameplay loop and readable silhouettes over flashy graphics.

To bring this aesthetic into the modern era, I integrated dynamic elements from the [Roguelike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike) genre. By utilizing [Procedural generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procedural_generation) for the mazes and unpredictable events, the game borrows from a lineage of titles that prioritize deep replayability and mastery over static memorization.

## Instructions (Master the Chaos)
* **Move:** Use the W, A, S, D keys or Arrow Keys to navigate the dangerous isometric grid.
* **Dash:** Press the Spacebar to dash! Use it to escape tight corners or outrun faster enemies!
* **Watch the UI:** Keep a close eye on the top left for your Score and current Chaos Event modifier.
* **Survive:** Do not let the ghosts touch you, and avoid the explosive bombs when chaos strikes! 💣

## Conclusion (Game Over, Or Is It?)
Green Panic is a brilliant testament to the idea that severe constraints breed supreme creativity. By limiting the color palette and relying strictly on vanilla web technologies, the focus remains entirely on crafting a tight, responsive, and thrilling user experience. It is a highly playable piece of digital art that honors the past while perfectly pushing browser capabilities to their limits! 🏆

### Theory (Sparks of Chaos)
The visual language is a direct, loving tribute to the glorious era of Nintendo's first major portable console. I wanted to capture that exact feeling of staring at a non-backlit screen, where the contrast between dark green and light green was your entire universe. The aesthetic is intentionally raw, celebrating the beauty of authentic [Pixel art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel_art).

Mechanically, the introduction of 'Chaos Events' was inspired by modern chaotic multiplayer games and the rising trend of livestream integrations where viewers can directly mess with the player. I wanted a single-player [Arcade game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_game) to feel like there was an unseen trickster pulling the strings, keeping the player constantly on their toes!

### Challenges (The Boss Battles)
* **Isometric Depth Sorting:** Drawing a 2.5D world requires rendering entities in the exact correct order (back to front) so players do not visually clip through walls!
* **Procedural Maze Logic:** Generating a maze randomly is easy, but generating a maze that is actually fun, solvable, and has no dead-end traps requires highly complex pathfinding algorithms.
* **AI Pathfinding:** Making ghosts smart enough to chase the player on a tricky isometric grid without getting eternally stuck on corners!

### Solutions (Leveling Up)
* Implemented a strict Z-index sorting algorithm based on the grid's Y and X coordinates before every single render frame to ensure absolutely perfect visual depth.
* Used a customized recursive backtracker algorithm to carve out the maze, combined with a validation pass to ensure open loops for fluid, non-stop movement.
* Equipped the enemy AI with a smart, localized [A* search algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A*_search_algorithm) variant, allowing them to calculate the shortest path to the player dynamically!

### Impact (High Score)
This project serves as a fantastic benchmark for what can be achieved with pure vanilla JavaScript and the Canvas API. For web developers, it provides a clean, dependency-free example of isometric rendering and smooth game loop management. For players, it offers a quick, highly accessible burst of entertainment that requires zero installation! ⚡

### Scope (Map Boundaries)
Green Panic is strictly an endless arcade survival game. It includes dynamic levels, smart enemies, and a robust rendering engine. It intentionally excludes complex level editors, multiplayer networking, or external assets like image files, keeping the entire codebase incredibly lightweight and instantly executable in any browser.

## Technical Details (Under the Hood)
Green Panic runs entirely on a custom-built JavaScript game engine! It cleverly uses the `requestAnimationFrame` API to maintain a buttery 60 frames per second. The isometric perspective is achieved by mathematically transforming standard 2D Cartesian coordinates into an angled grid using precise custom projection formulas. All graphics are drawn pixel-by-pixel using the Canvas context, ensuring the retro aesthetic is hardcoded straight into the logic! 📟

### Technologies
This project leverages the raw, incredible power of vanilla web tech to handle all logic and rendering without the overhead of heavy gaming frameworks.
* **JavaScript**: The absolute brain of the operation, handling game loops, AI behavior, and procedural generation.
* **HTML5 Canvas**: Used for high-performance, pixel-perfect rendering of the isometric world without relying on the DOM.
* **CSS**: Kept extremely minimal and nested, utilized to style the surrounding UI, scanlines, and ensure an immersive app-like experience.
