# Sidescrolling the Mountain
### Those Dreamy Pixelscapes

## TL;DR Version
Sidescrolling the Mountain is an endless platformer that looks, sounds, and feels exactly like a classic 90s video game. You jump between purple digital platforms, collect floating golden bits, and try to travel as far as possible without hitting the sharp spikes!

It features an awesome retro visual filter with scanlines to make it look like a vintage television set. Built with clever procedural generation, it guarantees you will never play the exact same mountain twice. You can even hook up your favorite controller and start setting high scores!

## Introduction (Welcome to the Pixelscape)
Step into a wave of pure nostalgia where the world moved slower and afternoons drifted seamlessly into the night. Revisit the childhood memories of hours spent on your living room couch, controller firmly in hand, facing the warm and fuzzy glow of the television. 🏔️

This creative digital experience is a love letter to the[8-bit computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-bit_computing) era. It is not just about reaching a final destination; it is about the simple joy of discovery and the beauty of the endless journey through a vibrant landscape of purple peaks and bright orange suns. Grab your controller, take a deep breath, and get ready to jump!

## Project Overview (The Endless Ascent)
Sidescrolling the Mountain is a procedural platforming adventure that serves as a beautiful tribute to classic arcade runners. It invites you to scale never-ending digital peaks, collecting bits while avoiding tricky obstacles. The environment shifts constantly, offering fresh, exciting challenges every single time you hit the start button. 👾

What makes this project truly special is its incredible dedication to an authentic [Cathode-ray tube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathode-ray_tube) visual aesthetic. By blending nostalgic visuals with incredibly smooth web performance, the game creates an immersive space designed for relaxation and rhythm. The mechanics feel instantly familiar, wrapping you in a cozy, digital embrace.

### Inspiration (The Geometry of Childhood)
This platforming adventure draws heavily from the mesmerizing [Vaporwave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaporwave) art style and the technical charm of the beloved [Nintendo Entertainment System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Entertainment_System). Conceptually, it explores the fascinating idea of a[Liminal Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liminal_space_(aesthetic)) - a magical, transitional zone that exists solely in our memories and compiled code. 🌅

By utilizing a clever endless generation algorithm, the mountain transforms into a metaphor for persistence and personal growth. The beautifully layered backgrounds pay direct homage to the brilliant [Parallax Scrolling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallax_scrolling) techniques used by legendary 90s developers to create vast worlds on hardware with strict limitations.

### Theory (The Nostalgic Architecture)
The intersection of low-resolution art and high-fidelity emotional connection creates a profoundly moving experience. Back in the day, the strict hardware limitations of early consoles dictated the art style, forcing brilliant creators to rely on bright palettes and clever coding workarounds.

This arcade project captures that exact spirit by intentionally restricting the color palette to deep, moody purples and warm, radiant oranges. The result is an environment that feels both deeply retro and eternally timeless, allowing players to completely lose themselves in the hypnotic rhythm of the jump.

### Challenges (Scaling the Logic)
* Creating a procedural algorithm that dynamically generates a truly endless, jumpable path without ever trapping the player or creating impossible leaps of faith.
* Managing multiple layers of background scenery at varying speeds while maintaining a locked, buttery-smooth 60 frames per second.
* Implementing a complex retro pixelation filter and scanline overlay that does not introduce frustrating input lag.
* Syncing the[Gamepad API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Gamepad_API) to perfectly match the tactile, immediate feel of a physical console controller.

### Solutions (Digital Triumphs)
* The world is brilliantly constructed in chunk-based segments, calculating platforms based on maximum jump distances to ensure flawless traversability.
* Each background layer is drawn with a specific offset relative to a global camera coordinate, forging a highly convincing sense of 3D depth.
* An inline SVG filter handles the visual pixelation, allowing the core code to run at high resolution for flawless collision detection.
* A custom unified input controller seamlessly listens to both keyboard and gamepad events, offering a flawless transition between control schemes.

### Impact (Emotional Resonance)
Sidescrolling the Mountain proves that technical constraints can be beautifully leveraged to evoke powerful, positive emotions. It offers a meditative and joyful escape for users, wrapped entirely in a high-performance, accessible web package. Players leave feeling refreshed, having spent a few delightful minutes reconnecting with their inner child. 🛸

### Scope (Journey Parameters)
This retro experience focuses entirely on continuous forward momentum and high-score chasing. It intentionally avoids complex combat or deep narratives to keep the gameplay loop pure, simple, and instantly accessible. The boundaries of the project are defined by its simplicity: jump, survive, and enjoy the gorgeous pixelated view.

## Technical Details (Under the Hood)
The custom platforming engine runs on a smooth Euler integration system for physics, handling gravity, acceleration, and friction perfectly. Instead of moving the player across the screen, a global camera offset is seamlessly applied to every draw call, keeping the hero centered while the magical digital world scrolls past! 💾

### Technologies
This retro dream beautifully leverages the raw power of the modern web to recreate the past!
* **[HTML5 Canvas](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Canvas_API)**: Used as the primary, lightning-fast rendering engine for all game sprites and procedural geometry.
* **JavaScript**: Powers the intelligent generation logic, the smooth player physics loop, and the complex multi-input handling.
* **CSS**: Manages the layered user interface, the glowing scanline overlays, and responsive, dynamic layouts.
* **SVG Filters**: Creates the signature, stunning 'Pixelate' effect that defines the project's entire visual identity.
