# Prismatico
### Your colors, your hues!

## TL;DR Version
Listen well, adventurer! Prismatico is a high-level artifact for your browser that turns the mundane task of picking colors into an epic quest. It features an **Alchemy Mixer** for blending gradients, a **Legibility Oracle** that rolls automatic Perception checks against WCAG standards, and a **Scribe's Desk** for exporting your palette to binary formats (ASE) or web spells (CSS). It handles the complex math (the arcane arts) so you can focus on the glory of your visual campaign.

## Introduction
Gather 'round the tavern fire, designers and developers! We have all faced the **Beholder of Bad Contrast** or gotten lost in the **Labyrinth of Hex Codes**. Too often, our tools are dull, lifeless blades that break when we strike against the hard armor of professional file formats.

I forged **Prismatico** in the fires of Mount DOM to be the weapon you deserve. It is not just a utility; it is a companion. It names your colors like legendary items found in a dragon's hoard—transforming `#FF4500` into "Orange Dragon" or `#10B981` into "Emerald Sage." It brings the flavor of a Friday night campaign to your Monday morning workflow.

## Project Overview
Prismatico is a Single Page Application (SPA) that functions as a Guild Hall for your colors. At its heart lies the **Crystal Core** (a reactive state management system) that tracks your party's inventory (the palette) and your history (the quest log).

The interface uses a **Glassmorphism** aesthetic to resemble a scrying pool. You interact with the "Saturation Pad" to channel raw mana into specific hues. The standout feature is the **Binary Transmutation Engine**, which allows this web-based tool to speak the ancient tongue of desktop applications, parsing and generating `.ase` and `.aco` files byte-by-byte. It is a bridge between the browser realm and the Adobe kingdom.

## Instructions (How to Wield the Artifact)
1.  **Channeling:** Drag the rune on the main pad to adjust your Light and Saturation. Use the Hue slider to shift your elemental alignment.
2.  **Naming:** The Oracle will automatically bestow a legendary name (e.g., "Mithril Cloud") upon your selection.
3.  **The Perception Check:** Navigate to the **Legibility** tab. Green badges mean you passed the WCAG saving throw; Red means you critically failed.
4.  **Alchemy:** Enter the **Mixer**. Select a Start and End color, then cast "Generate" to create a bridge of potions between them.
5.  **Looting:** Open the "Download" satchel in the footer. Choose your format to scribe your inventory for other realms.

### Theory (Arcane Lore)
The magic of Prismatico is fueled by the **[HSL Color Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSL_and_HSV)**. Unlike the chaotic RGB model (which is like trying to mix potions in the dark), HSL separates the *Soul* (Hue) from the *Body* (Saturation and Lightness). This allows for "Harmonic Resonance"—calculating complementary colors by simply rotating the Hue wheel by 180 degrees.

The **[WCAG 2.1 Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/)** serve as the dungeon rules. The algorithm calculates the **Relative Luminance** of your color (how much light it emits) and compares it to text colors. This is a pure Math check: `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)`. If the result is lower than 4.5, the check fails, and your users suffer the "Blinded" condition.

For the **[Procedural Generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procedural_generation)** of names, the code acts as a Dungeon Master tables. It checks the "Lightness" stat: if high, it pulls from the "Celestial" adjective pool; if low, it draws from the "Abyssal" pool.

### Challenges (The Curses)
*   **The Curse of Endianness:** Writing binary files for Adobe (`.ase`) requires Big-Endian byte order, but the modern web browser (the Player Character) speaks Little-Endian. This required a translation layer using `DataView`.
*   **The Mobile Mimic:** Touch events on the canvas often mimic mouse events but behave wildly differently, causing the color cursor to jump like a teleporting goblin.
*   **The Alpha Ghost:** Floating point math in JavaScript is notorious for errors (e.g., `0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3`), which caused the transparency slider to return values like `0.999999994`.

### Solutions (The Counter-Spells)
*   **Manual Byte Scribing:** I wrote a custom binary writer that places bytes into the buffer manually, ensuring the Adobe Realm accepts our offering.
*   **Unified Input Handler:** A master function `mv(e)` that detects whether the input comes from a mouse or a touch screen and normalizes the coordinates relative to the canvas hitbox.
*   **Rounding Wards:** Applied strict `toFixed(2)` and rounding logic at the input stage to banish the floating-point ghosts before they could haunt the state management.

### Impact (XP Gained)
Prismatico levels up the entire design workflow. It grants the user:
*   **+10 Efficiency:** No more tabbing between three different websites to build a palette.
*   **+5 Accessibility:** Passive perception checks ensure no design leaves the table without being readable.
*   **Cross-Realm Travel:** The ability to export native files allows the palette to travel from the web browser to Photoshop or Illustrator instantly.

### Scope (Map Boundaries)
This campaign is strictly for **Color Management**. It is not a painting canvas (MS Paint) nor a vector tool (Figma). It is a specialized inventory management system for hues, tints, and shades.

## Technical Details (The Stat Block)
The artifact is constructed using **Vanilla JavaScript**—no frameworks, no libraries, just pure elemental code. It uses a custom "State" object that acts as the character sheet, tracking `h`, `s`, `l`, `a`, and the `palette` inventory.

The application utilizes the **[Canvas API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Canvas_API)** to render the saturation gradient dynamically. It employs **[Bitwise Operators](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Operators/Bitwise_AND)** in the binary parser to shift bytes correctly for file exports.

### Technologies
This project leverages the power of **[Web Standards]** to cast its spells.
*   **[Canvas API]**: For rendering the high-performance color picker pad.
*   **[DataView & ArrayBuffer]**: For low-level binary manipulation of ASE/ACO files.
*   **[CSS Custom Properties]**: For the dynamic theming that reacts to the user's color choice.
*   **[LocalStorage]**: To save the player's progress between sessions.

### Future Improvements
*   **Cloud Saves:** A "Bag of Holding" in the cloud to sync palettes across devices.
*   **Multiplayer Mode:** Real-time collaboration on a palette with other party members via WebSockets.
*   **Pixel Art Converter:** A feature to upload an image and "de-res" it into a limited palette for pixel art.
